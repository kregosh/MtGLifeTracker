Privacy policy

MtG Life Tracker · last updated 20 October 2026

MtG Life Tracker is a free life counter for Magic: The Gathering games, shared live between the players' phones and browsers. This page explains what the app stores, where, and why. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is sent to our game server

Games are synchronised through Google Firebase (Realtime Database and anonymous Authentication). While you play, it stores:

  • an anonymous ID that Firebase creates for your installation. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account;
  • the display name you choose (it can be anything);
  • your game state: life total, the counters you track (such as commander damage or poison), whether you conceded, and whether you are connected;
  • the session's code, rules and timer settings;
  • if you use friends: friend requests (your ID and display name, sent to the player you add) and which session you are currently in, so your friends can join you.

Everyone in a session sees the names and game state of the players in it. Sessions are deleted when the last player leaves. A game nobody left properly (for example because a phone ran out of battery) can stay on the server, with the names and counters in it, until it is cleaned up; you can ask for it to be deleted at any time (see below).

What stays on your device

Your display name, settings, chosen background picture, friends list and the players you recently played with are kept only on your device. A background picture you choose is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Permissions

  • Internet, to synchronise the game.
  • Notifications and foreground service, to keep a game connected for a while when you switch to another app, with a notification showing it is running.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Google processes the data above only to run Firebase for the app; see Firebase's privacy information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can leave a session at any time, which removes your seat from it. To have anything else deleted, or for any question about this policy, open an issue at github.com/kregosh/MtGLifeTracker/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
